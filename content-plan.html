04 — Build My Content Plan

Turn one content idea into a simple plan that tells you what to create, who it is for and what value it should provide.

🎯 Your Content Direction

Niche: Your niche

Audience: Your audience

Format: Short video

Goal: Teach something useful

📝 Create Your Plan

🧩 Simple Content Structure

1. Hook

Give the audience a reason to continue watching or reading.

2. Value

Deliver the useful information, explanation, demonstration or story.

3. Clear ending

Summarize the main point and give an appropriate next step.

💡 EarnFlow principle:

A content plan does not need to be complicated. The goal is to make it easier to consistently create useful content instead of waiting for inspiration.
