Choose Your Digital Product

Start with a useful problem. Your product should help a specific person achieve something, understand something or complete a task more easily.

💡 What Is a Digital Product?

A digital product is something useful that can be delivered electronically instead of as a physical item.

Examples include educational guides, templates, planners, checklists, worksheets and other useful digital resources.

📦 Product Examples

📘 Guide A practical resource explaining how to do something.
📋 Checklist A structured list that helps someone complete a task.
📝 Template A ready-to-use document that saves the customer time.
📅 Planner A resource for organizing activities, goals or tasks.
📊 Tracker A tool for recording progress, activities or results.
📚 Workbook A resource containing explanations and practical exercises.

🎯 Start With a Problem

Instead of asking only, "What can I sell?", ask:

"What useful problem can I help someone solve?"

A strong starting point is a problem that people regularly experience and would find useful to solve.

👥 Identify Your Customer

Your product becomes easier to design when you know who it is for.

🧠 Find a Product Idea

Choose the type of resource that best fits the problem.

💡 EarnFlow principle:

Start small. A simple product that solves one clear problem can be easier to understand, create and improve than a huge product trying to solve everything.

⚠️ Important:

Don't copy another creator's product, branding or copyrighted material. Build something based on your own knowledge, research and useful ideas.
