03 — Generate Content Ideas

Turn your niche and audience knowledge into practical content ideas you can develop into useful posts, videos or educational resources.

🎯 Your Content Direction

Niche: Your niche

Audience: Your audience

🧠 Build Your Ideas

🧩 Content Idea Formula

Audience + Problem + Useful Outcome

Start with something your audience cares about, identify a problem or question and create content that provides a useful outcome.

Example

Instead of: "5 things about smartphones."

Try: "5 smartphone settings beginners should check today."

💡 EarnFlow principle:

You don't need hundreds of ideas before you start. One useful idea developed well is more valuable than a long list of ideas that never become actual content.

⚠️ Avoid clickbait deception:

A strong title can attract attention, but the content should actually deliver what the title promises.
