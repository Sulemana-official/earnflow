Do Good Work

A practical delivery guide for completing freelance work professionally and keeping your customers informed.

📋 Your Delivery Checklist

Before delivering your work, go through each step below.

Confirm exactly what the customer asked for.
Confirm what is included in the agreed service.
Confirm the deadline and delivery method.
Complete the work carefully.
Review your work for obvious mistakes.
Make sure the final files are organized and usable.
Deliver the agreed work clearly.
Ask whether the customer has any questions or agreed revisions.
01

Confirm the Requirements

Before starting, make sure you understand what the customer actually wants.

Clarify important details such as the type of work, quantity, format, deadline and any specific instructions.

02

Agree on the Work

Make sure both sides understand what you are providing before you begin.

A clear agreement helps prevent misunderstandings about the scope of the project.

03

Communicate Clearly

If something changes or you encounter a problem, communicate with the customer instead of disappearing.

Professional communication can help maintain trust throughout the project.

04

Review Your Work

Before sending the final work, check it carefully.

Look for spelling mistakes, missing files, incorrect information, poor formatting or anything else that could have been avoided.

05

Deliver What You Promised

Send the agreed work using the agreed delivery method.

Don't claim that you completed something that you did not complete.

06

Handle Revisions Professionally

If revisions were part of the agreement, handle them according to what you originally agreed with the customer.

If a customer requests work outside the original agreement, clarify the additional requirements before proceeding.

07

Ask for Feedback

After completing the work, you can politely ask the customer whether everything is satisfactory and whether they have useful feedback.

💡 EarnFlow principle:

Good freelance work isn't only about completing a task. It's also about understanding the customer's needs, communicating clearly and delivering what you agreed to provide.

⚠️ Protect yourself:

Keep records of important agreements, deadlines, files and customer instructions. Don't share passwords, sensitive personal information or unnecessary account access.
