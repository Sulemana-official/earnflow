01 — Choose Your Content Niche

A focused niche makes it easier to decide what to create, who you are creating for and what type of value your content provides.

🎯 What Is a Content Niche?

A niche is a focused subject or audience that your content regularly serves.

You don't have to choose the perfect niche immediately. Start with something you can learn about, create useful content around and continue improving.

💡 Choose a Starting Direction

Tap an option that interests you most.

📚 Education Study tips, explanations and learning resources.
📱 Technology Apps, smartphones, software and useful technology tips.
💰 Finance Education Budgeting, saving and general financial education.
💼 Career Skills CVs, interviews, workplace skills and career development.
🎬 Content Creation Video creation, editing, storytelling and creator skills.
🚀 Business Skills Small-business education, marketing and useful business tools.
🧠 Personal Development Productivity, habits, learning and personal growth.
✨ Other Another useful subject you want to explore.

🧭 Define Your Direction

💡 EarnFlow principle:

Don't choose a niche only because you think it will make money. Choose a subject where you can consistently learn, create useful content and serve a specific audience.

⚠️ Important:

Avoid misleading claims, fake results and copied content. Build your audience by providing genuine value.
