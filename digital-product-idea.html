💡 How to Find a Strong Product Idea

Problem first.

Look for something people struggle with, repeatedly ask about or spend time trying to solve.

Then ask whether a simple digital resource could make that problem easier.

⚠️ Important

Don't copy another person's product and present it as your own.

Don't make false claims about what your product can achieve.

Your first idea doesn't have to be perfect. You can improve it after learning more about your customer.
