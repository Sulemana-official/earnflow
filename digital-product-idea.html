🛠️ Build the First Version

01 — Keep it focused Start by solving the single problem you identified instead of trying to build a huge product.
02 — Create useful content Give the customer practical information, steps, examples, templates or exercises that help them reach the desired outcome.
03 — Make it easy to use Organize the product clearly so the customer can understand what to do next.
04 — Test it Let a suitable person review the product and look for confusing instructions, missing information or unnecessary complexity.
05 — Improve it Use useful feedback to improve future versions rather than trying to make everything perfect immediately.

🎯 Your First Version Plan

A simple first version could follow this structure:

Problem Explain the problem clearly.
Solution Show the customer how your resource helps solve it.
Steps Give practical steps the customer can follow.
Examples Include examples where they make the idea easier to understand.
Action Tell the customer what to do next.
💡 EarnFlow principle:

Your first version doesn't need to be enormous. Focus on making it genuinely useful for the person you are trying to help.

⚠️ Important:

Before selling a product, make sure your content is original or properly licensed and that any claims you make are accurate.
