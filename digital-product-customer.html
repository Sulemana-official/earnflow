💡 Customer Rule

Specific beats broad.

Instead of saying "students", consider "SHS students preparing for WASSCE who struggle to organize revision."

A clearer customer makes it easier to decide what to build and how to explain its value.

⚠️ Important

Don't invent customer testimonials, sales numbers or research results.

This step is a planning tool. Later, you can validate your assumptions by speaking with real potential customers or observing genuine demand.
