💰 Possible Pricing Direction

📝 Final Planning Note

💡 EarnFlow principle

Plan enough to build a useful first version. You do not need to create everything at once. Build, learn from real users, then improve.

⚠️ Avoid overbuilding

Do not spend large amounts of money or months building features before you have evidence that customers actually need them.
