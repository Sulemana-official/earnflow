🧱 Build Your First Version

Work through these tasks in order. The goal is to create something useful—not something unnecessarily large.

1️⃣ Set Up the Product

Create the main document, workbook, template, course area or other format you selected in your product plan.

2️⃣ Build the Core Content

Create the content for each planned section. Focus on practical information, examples and instructions that help the customer achieve the intended outcome.

3️⃣ Add Useful Resources

Add the worksheets, checklists, templates, examples, prompts or other resources included in your plan.

4️⃣ Make It Easy to Use

Organize the product clearly. Customers should understand where to start, what to do next and how to use each resource.

5️⃣ Check the Customer Outcome

Compare your product with the outcome you promised to provide. Remove content that does not help the customer reach that outcome.

6️⃣ Remove Unnecessary Features

Do not add features simply because they sound impressive. Keep Version 1 focused on solving the customer's main problem.

💡 EarnFlow principle

Build a useful first version before trying to make it perfect. Real customer feedback can show you what needs improvement.

⚠️ Avoid copying

Use your own original work or properly licensed material. Do not copy another creator's paid product, copyrighted content or branding.
