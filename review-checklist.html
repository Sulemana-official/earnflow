Review Your Work

Check your work carefully before delivering it to your customer.

✅ Final Review

Go through each item before you send your work.

0% complete
📋 Requirements checked
Confirm that you completed the work according to the customer's instructions and the agreed scope.
🔤 Spelling and grammar checked
Review written content for obvious spelling, grammar and typing mistakes.
🎨 Quality checked
Look at the final work carefully and make sure it meets the quality level you intended to provide.
📁 Files organized
Make sure the correct files are included, clearly named and easy for the customer to understand.
📱 Tested where possible
If your work is a website, document, design or other digital product, test it where practical before delivery.
⏰ Deadline checked
Confirm that you are delivering within the deadline you agreed with the customer.
🔒 Privacy checked
Remove unnecessary private information, passwords or sensitive data before sending files.
💬 Customer instructions followed
Check the customer's important instructions one final time before delivery.
📦 Final delivery prepared
Make sure everything the customer is supposed to receive is ready to send.
🎉 Final review complete!

You've checked all the items. You can now prepare your final delivery.

Remember to communicate clearly with the customer and deliver only what was agreed.

💡 EarnFlow principle:

Taking a few minutes to review your work can help you catch mistakes before the customer receives it.

⚠️ Important:

A completed checklist does not guarantee that the work is perfect. Use your own judgment and review the actual requirements of every project.
