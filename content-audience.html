🔎 Learn About Your Audience

01 — Listen to real questions Pay attention to questions people ask about your niche. Those questions can become future content topics.
02 — Study useful feedback Look for recurring comments, questions and suggestions that reveal what your audience finds confusing or useful.
03 — Don't assume everyone is your customer A focused audience is often easier to understand and serve than trying to create content for everybody.
04 — Solve problems, not just trends Trends can attract attention, but useful problem-solving content can build longer-term value.

💡 Audience Question Bank

Use these questions when researching your audience:

  • What are they trying to learn?
  • What mistakes do they commonly make?
  • What takes too much time?
  • What confuses them?
  • What result are they trying to achieve?
  • What information is difficult for them to find?
💡 EarnFlow principle:

The goal isn't to manipulate people into watching. The goal is to understand their needs well enough to create content that genuinely helps them.

⚠️ Protect your audience:

Don't collect unnecessary personal information from viewers. Respect privacy and avoid making unsupported claims about your audience.
