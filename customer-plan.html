🔍 What to Look For

They appear to have a genuine need for my service.
I can clearly explain how my service could help them.
I can find a legitimate way to contact them.
I have researched them before contacting them.

📝 Research Before Contacting

Look at what the person or business does. Check their existing work and identify one specific problem your service could help solve.

💬 Prepare Your Approach

Keep your first message short and personalized. Explain why you are contacting them and focus on how your service could be useful.

🚫 Avoid Spam

Do not send the same message to hundreds of people. Respect people's decisions and stop contacting someone if they are not interested.

💡 Remember: Finding a potential customer is only the beginning. Your goal is to build trust, communicate clearly and provide useful work.
