Validate Your Product Idea

Before spending a lot of time creating your product, check whether the problem, customer and value are clear.

🎯 Your Product Idea

📊 Validation Questions

Answer honestly. The score is only a simple decision aid—it is not proof that a product will sell.

1. Is the problem clear and specific?
2. Can you clearly identify people who experience this problem?
3. Does your product provide a useful outcome?
4. Have you found evidence that people are interested in this problem?
5. Can you create a useful first version without making it unnecessarily large?
6. Can you explain the value of the product in one simple sentence?

🔎 Simple Demand Research

Before creating the full product, look for real evidence that the problem matters.

Ask potential customers:

  • Do you experience this problem?
  • How do you currently solve it?
  • What is difficult about your current solution?
  • What would make a better solution useful?

You can also examine relevant discussions, search results, communities and existing products to understand the problem better.

💡 EarnFlow principle:

Validation isn't about guaranteeing sales. It is about reducing avoidable mistakes by learning about the problem and customer before investing heavily in the product.

⚠️ Important:

Never invent testimonials, customer demand, sales numbers or research results to make a product appear more successful than it is.
