Validate Your Product Idea

Before building the full product, check whether the customer, problem, value and demand are clear enough to justify moving forward.

🎯 Your Product Idea

📊 Validation Questions

Answer honestly. This score is a simple decision aid, not proof that your product will sell.

1. Is the problem clear and specific?
2. Can you clearly identify people who experience this problem?
3. Does your product provide a useful outcome?
4. Have you found evidence that people are interested in this problem?
5. Can you create a useful first version without making it unnecessarily large?
6. Can you explain the value of the product in one simple sentence?

🔎 Simple Demand Research

Before creating the full product, look for real evidence that the problem matters.

Ask potential customers:

  • Do you experience this problem?
  • How do you currently solve it?
  • What is difficult about your current solution?
  • What would make a better solution useful?
  • Would you consider paying for a better solution?

You can also examine relevant discussions, search results, communities and existing products to understand the problem and the alternatives people already use.

💡 EarnFlow principle

Validation is not about guaranteeing sales. It is about reducing avoidable mistakes by learning about the customer and problem before investing heavily in the product.

⚠️ Important

Never invent testimonials, customer demand, sales numbers or research results to make a product appear more successful than it really is.
